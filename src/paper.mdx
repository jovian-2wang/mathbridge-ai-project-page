  <h2 id="abstract-title">Abstract</h2>
  <p>Students often need more than a verdict that an answer is wrong. MathBridge AI connects a curriculum task with the student's attempt, learning preferences, and evidence from earlier work. It uses that context to offer a next step—a hint, visual, or related problem—while keeping the mathematical relationship in view. Teachers can inspect skill-level evidence; families can see a simpler account of the same learning activity. The current prototype focuses on selected Grade 6 mathematics tasks.</p>
